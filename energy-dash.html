Exploring the Impact of the U.S. Data Center Boom: An Interactive Dashboard

Data centers are the physical infrastructure behind everything digital, yet they've stayed largely out of sight — until recently. This project set out to capture how Americans are reacting to the data center boom and what the future might hold.

01 · Challenge

What began as a look at AI policy across levels of government turned into a broader exploration of the data center boom sweeping the U.S. As construction accelerates, it's drawing scrutiny from utility regulators, environmental advocates, and local governments — and pushback is spreading at the municipal level, from neighborhood votes against proposals to construction moratoriums to renewed federal interest in AI regulation.

02 · Solution

To capture the many angles of the data-center/energy relationship in one place, I built an interactive dashboard in R using shiny, tidyverse, sf, tigris, dplyr, and leaflet.

Explore the live dashboard ↗

03 · Process

Media audit

Recurring themes across media coverage: AI literacy, data-center energy use and regulation efforts, AI tool adoption and its opponents, and where new construction is concentrated along with resident pushback.

Outreach & literature review

I reached out to individuals and organizations tracking local data-center activity and AI policy to hear first-hand accounts, and reviewed existing research to better understand key players, trends, and regulatory efforts.

Data exploration & analysis

Key sources: the U.S. Energy Information Administration (residential prices and volatility), Business Insider (confirmed data center locations), and the CDC/ATSDR 2022 Social Vulnerability Index.

04 · Reflection & Findings

Staying within scope was a real challenge given how much legal and cultural nuance exists between states, cities, and towns — the project ultimately required a lot of flexibility, following the research where it led rather than the original AI-policy framing.

An interactive dashboard felt like the right format for a topic with this many layers. The connection between Big Tech's AI push and data center construction is becoming clearer to the public, even as companies like Oracle, Amazon, and Microsoft conduct layoffs while investing heavily in AI — a contrast that's fueling public wariness about AI's promised benefits.
