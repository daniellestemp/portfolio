About

I am a graduate student working toward a Master of Science in Data Analytics & Visualization at Pratt Institute (Manhattan, New York). Much of my work in my graduate studies has surrounded government data and public policy research, and I’m interested in pursuing a career within policy research, data journalism, or government affairs.

My professional background is in technology and financial public relations. Throughout my years working with tech/finance start-ups, consultants, and media, part of my role was to leverage various client data to tell dynamic stories and create meaningful content. That was one of my favorite aspects of the job and I decided I wanted to pursue a profession in data more seriously.

I have completed my Bachelor of Science in Political Science and Advertising/Public Relations from the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT) in Rochester, New York. I grew up near Milwaukee, Wisconsin.
